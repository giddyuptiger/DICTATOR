Dictator turns your voice into clean, formatted text in any app — messages, email, notes, anywhere you can type. Tap the mic on the Dictator keyboard, speak, and your words land where your cursor is.

WHY DICTATOR

- Private by default. Your speech is transcribed right on your iPhone, so your voice never has to leave your device. It even works offline.

- Actually clean text. Dictator doesn't just transcribe. It fixes punctuation and capitalization, drops the "um"s and false starts, and breaks long thoughts into real paragraphs. You get text you'd have typed — not a raw dump.

- Works everywhere. A keyboard you can use in any app on iPhone, plus a hold-to-talk companion for your Mac. Text appears wherever you're typing.

- No subscription. On-device dictation is free — no $15 a month, no account, no ads, no tracking.

Dictator records over whatever you're playing. It never pauses, ducks, or hijacks your music or car audio.

HOW IT WORKS

Because iOS keyboards can't use the microphone directly, Dictator listens in its own app and keeps the mic ready in the background, so dictation is instant. Your audio is transcribed on your device; a fast, secure formatting step polishes the text. No account required.
